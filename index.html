Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026 · IRLmartin Lab, Dublin, Ireland

IRLmartin Lab ("we", "Martin") helps TikTok Shop creators and sellers in the United States and United Kingdom understand enforcement actions, prepare appeals and keep their accounts compliant. Most of that work happens inside our Discord server and through our Discord application (the "assistant"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the control you have over it.

In short. We hold what you give us in your private case room, use it only to work on your case, process it with the services listed below, and delete it when you ask. We never ask for your password or login codes, we never log in to your account, and we never act on your account.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is IRLmartin Lab, operated by Martin Nealon, Dublin, Ireland. Contact: irlmartinbusiness@gmail.com, or message staff inside the IRLmartin Lab Discord.

2. What we collect

  • Discord account details: your Discord user ID, username and display name, the roles you pick (market, account type, GMV band) and the date you joined.
  • Case information you provide: messages, answers to the assistant's questions, screenshots and files you upload in your private case room, such as enforcement notices, Creator Health Rating pages, device lists, connected-app lists, appeal confirmations and support-chat transcripts.
  • TikTok account data you choose to share. If you connect your TikTok account or authorise a data export during a case, we receive only the data TikTok shows you on its consent screen. For TikTok Shop connections this is your creator profile and recent affiliate order summary. For data exports this is your activity, posts and profile. We use it solely to verify your account and investigate your case. Login history is reduced to device, network, carrier and date; IP addresses are masked and the original file is not stored. We delete any access token as soon as the data has been read, and we delete the summary when your case closes or on request. We never receive your password, and we never post, message or change anything on your account.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe. We receive confirmation that a payment or subscription succeeded, the amount, and a reference to your case. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Service records: case status, timestamps, documents the assistant produces for you (evidence briefs, appeal drafts, reports) and anonymised aggregate statistics such as win rate.

3. Why we use it and on what basis

  • To deliver the service you asked for: assessing your situation, preparing evidence and appeals, and supporting your account (performance of a contract).
  • To run and protect the Discord server, including warning members about impersonators and preventing scams (legitimate interests).
  • To read TikTok data you authorise (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time).
  • To improve how we handle future cases, using anonymised and aggregated outcomes only (legitimate interests).

We do not sell personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with other clients.

4. Who processes it for us

  • Discord hosts the server and messages.
  • Railway hosts the assistant and its database (United States).
  • Google Gemini API analyses text and images you share in a case so the assistant can read notices, extract dates and draft documents. Data is sent for processing only and is not used to train models under the API terms.
  • Stripe processes payments.
  • TikTok provides account data only when you authorise it through TikTok's own consent screen.
  • GitHub hosts these pages.

Some of these providers are outside the EEA and UK. Transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses and data-protection frameworks.

5. How long we keep it

  • Case room content stays while your case is open. When it closes, a transcript is kept in a staff-only channel so Martin can support you if the issue returns.
  • TikTok access tokens are deleted immediately after use. Data-export summaries are deleted when the case closes.
  • Database backups are kept for 30 days and then overwritten.
  • Anonymised statistics contain no personal data and may be kept indefinitely.

6. Your control

Inside the Discord server, run /my-data at any time to see and download what we hold about you, and use the delete button there to erase it. You can also email us. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our use of your data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent for any TikTok data you authorised. If you are in the EEA or UK you may complain to your data protection authority; in Ireland that is the Data Protection Commission.

7. Security

Case rooms are visible only to you and IRLmartin Lab staff. Payment links are only ever shared inside private rooms. The assistant never asks for passwords, verification codes or identity documents; if a request like that ever appears to come from us, treat it as an impersonation attempt and report it to staff. Secrets used by the assistant are stored in the hosting provider's encrypted configuration, not in code or chat.

8. Children

Our services are for TikTok Shop account holders, who must be adults under TikTok's rules. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new effective date and announce material changes in the Discord server.

IRLmartin Lab is independent and not affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance. Martin never sends the first direct message. His only accounts are TikTok @iamjustthatguy33 and the IRLmartin Lab Discord.
